Regional-level (state / province / region, not city-specific)

  • In Ghana, type‑2 diabetes affects approximately six percent of adults, a percentage that is expected to rise. [1]
  • In Ghana, studies estimate the prevalence of diabetes to be between 2.6% and 9%. [1]
  • In Ghana, the Ghana Health Service records an average of 200,000 diabetes cases reported to health facilities annually. [1]
  • In Ghana, diabetes is now number seven. [1] (partially supported)

National-level (not city-specific)

  • In Africa, 24 million adults are currently living with diabetes, a number projected to rise by 129% to 55 million by 2045. [1]
  • In Africa, diabetes mellitus caused 416,000 deaths in 2022 and is expected to become one of the leading causes of death by 2030. [1]
  • In Ghana, the prevalence of hypertension is 30.3 % [2]
  • In Ghana, 52 % of heart failure admissions were associated with hypertension [2]
  • In Ghana, 35 % of persons with hypertension know their status [2]
  • In Ghana, 22 % of persons with hypertension are on treatment [2]
  • In Ghana, 6 % of persons with hypertension have their blood pressure controlled [2]
  • In Ghana, 90 % of patients cannot afford the cost of heart surgery [2]
  • The Ghana Heart Initiative (GHI) is an ongoing national programme to improve cardiovascular care in Ghana [2]
