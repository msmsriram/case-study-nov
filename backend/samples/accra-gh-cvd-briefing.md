## Health system capacity

*Understanding public and private facilities, cardiology services and insurance coverage informs where the city can strengthen service delivery.*


**City-level**

**State-level**

- Greater Accra Region has a high number of health workers per capita due to the concentration of doctors in the capital. [5] *(geography corrected by fact checker)*

**National-level (not city-specific)**

- In Ghana, the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) active membership reached 18.5 million in the last quarter of 2025, representing 56 percent of the country’s total population coverage. [4] *(2025)*
- In Ghana, the NHIS recorded 28.11 million facility visits across 5,449 health facilities nationwide in 2025. [4] *(2025)*
- In Ghana, the NHIS aims to reach 21 million members in 2025, expected to increase national coverage to about 62 percent. [4] *(2025)*
- In Ghana, the NHIS set a target of 20.8 million active members by the end of 2024, which was marginally missed. [4] *(2024)*
- In Ghana, as of 2014, the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) covered 10.5 million people, representing 40 percent of the country’s population. [5] *(2014)*
- The Ghanaian government has made substantial progress toward its goal of universal health care, as evidenced by the expansion of the NHIS. [5] *(2003)*
- In Ghana, inequalities persist across geography, gender, and income levels in NHIS access, requiring strategic collaboration among stakeholders. [4]
- In Ghana, over 95% of disease conditions that afflict the population are covered by the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS). [6]
- The NHIS provides outpatient services for hypertension, diabetes mellitus, and other non-communicable diseases such as asthma and rheumatism. [6]
- The NHIS covers emergency medical and surgical interventions, including heart surgery for accidents. [6]
- The NHIS includes echocardiography as a covered diagnostic service for heart conditions. [6]
- The National Health Insurance Scheme is a major source of operational financing for health facilities in Ghana. [5]
