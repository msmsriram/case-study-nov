- In Ghana, the National Non communicable Disease Policy (NNCDP, 2022) was revised. [6] *(partially supported; 2022)*
- In Ghana, the President launched an international Non-Communicable Diseases Compact 2022-2030 to accelerate progress towards the prevention and control of NCDs. [6] *(2022)*

- In Ghana, the Ministry of Health enacted Legislative Instrument 2247 in January 2017 to regulate and prevent tobacco use. [8] *(2017)*
- The LI 2247 prohibited the advertisement of tobacco. [8] *(partially supported; 2017)*
- Legislative Instrument 2247 mandates that tobacco packaging, labelling and health warnings be carried out and specifies the places where tobacco can be sold. [8] *(2017)*
- Non‑compliance with Legislative Instrument 2247 attracts a penalty of 750 penalty units, equivalent to GHȼ9,000.00. [8] *(2017)*
